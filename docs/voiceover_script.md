# 🎙️ Standalone Voiceover Script (Summary-First Hook Pitch)
## Project: Facial Recognition Monitoring Attendance System (FRMAS)

This script is professionally structured starting with the **Project Summary as the ultimate hook**, followed by the Project Title, Profile, Agency, Work Plan, and User Interface Prototype in exact order.

---

## ⏱️ Script Timeline & Spoken Pace
* **Target Duration**: 5 minutes (300 seconds)
* **Total Word Count**: ~765 words (perfectly paced at ~145 words per minute).
* **Key Cues**:
  - `[Tone]` indicates the emotional delivery.
  - `[...]` indicates a brief natural pause (1-2 seconds) to let the video catch up.
  - `(ACTION)` indicates what you should be doing on screen while speaking.

## 🎤 Tips for Reading Aloud

1. **Vocal Pacing**: Use the timestamps as a guide. 140 words per minute is a professional, relaxed pacing. Do not rush.
2. **Audio Sync**: Memorize your click sequence or record your screen first, then record the voiceover to match the visual timeline perfectly.
3. **Computer Volume**: Turn up your speakers during Phase 4 so the terminal's native speech synthesis `"Time In Successful"` is recorded clearly.
